Bin Day map maker

Pick a real neighbourhood to do your rounds in. The streets come from OpenStreetMap; the houses, bins and trees are random.

1. Find a place

2. Choose the area

Drag the corners or centre of the orange box, or draw a new one.

3. Preview


Neighbourhoods are sometimes only joined by a main road.

street house bin start

4. Save

Only the streets are saved. Each game places houses and bins afresh; press N on the title screen for a different lot.

Notes

Lanes, car parks and private roads are left out, and so are main roads unless you ask for them. Divided roads keep one carriageway, and small roundabouts become plain junctions. Streets that cross the edge of the box end in a turning circle.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Streets via the Overpass API, search via Nominatim.
